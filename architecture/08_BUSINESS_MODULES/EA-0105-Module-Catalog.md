# Module Catalog

## Purpose
The official registry of planned and active Business Modules in Campus OS, categorized by their overarching Business Domain.

## Official Domains & Modules

### 1. Foundation Domain
Modules providing transversal support across all business units.
- **Identity**: Manages user profiles, roles, and SSO integration.
- **Organization**: Manages structural hierarchies (Faculties, Departments, Campuses).
- **People**: Core demographic and biographic data for all persons (Students, Staff, Alumni).
- **Tenant**: Manages multi-tenant configurations and limits.
- **Notification**: Centralized delivery (Email, SMS, Push).
- **Document**: Secure storage, generation, and signing of official documents.

### 2. Academic Domain
Modules managing the core educational lifecycle.
- **Admission**: Application processing, scoring, and acceptance.
- **Student**: Enrollment status, academic records, and progression.
- **Curriculum**: Program structures, degree requirements, and syllabi.
- **Course**: Class scheduling, cataloging, and capacity management.
- **Teaching**: Educator assignments, workload, and evaluation.
- **Enrollment**: Course registration and section assignments.
- **Assessment**: Grading, exams, and rubrics.
- **Graduation**: Auditing degree requirements and commencement.

### 3. Research Domain
Modules managing the research lifecycle and intellectual output.
- **Research**: Proposal tracking, ethics review, and project lifecycle.
- **Grant**: Funding applications, awards, and financial compliance.
- **Publication**: Tracking books, articles, and citations.
- **Journal**: Internal journal management and peer review.
- **Conference**: Academic event organization and proceedings.
- **Repository**: Open-access institutional archives.
- **Intellectual Property**: Patents, copyrights, and technology transfer.

### 4. Certification Domain
Modules managing verifiable credentials and lifelong learning.
- **Certification**: Professional and non-degree certificate programs.
- **Examination**: Proctored testing and secure exam delivery.
- **Credential**: Generation of physical and digital certificates.
- **Digital Badge**: OpenBadges standard compliance.
- **CPD (Continuing Professional Development)**: Tracking lifelong learning credits.

### 5. Community Domain
Modules for external engagement.
- **Alumni**: Network management, giving, and career tracking.
- **Community Engagement**: Public service tracking.
- **Collaboration**: Joint programs with other institutions.
- **Partnership**: Managing corporate and academic MOUs.

### 6. Enterprise Domain
Modules for institutional operations.
- **Finance**: Billing, payments, ledgers, and budgeting.
- **Procurement**: Purchasing and vendor management.
- **Human Resources**: Staffing, payroll, and benefits.
- **Asset Management**: Facilities, equipment, and space planning.
- **Project Management**: Internal strategic initiatives.
