# Architecture Lifecycle

## Purpose
Defines the official, unalterable lifecycle model for all architectural artifacts within Campus OS, ensuring that every piece of the system has a defined beginning, middle, and end.

## The 8-Stage Architecture Lifecycle

### 1. Vision
- Definition of the business problem and the high-level Enterprise Strategy required to solve it.

### 2. Architecture
- The conceptual blueprint. The creation of the Business Architecture, Data Architecture, and Kernel definitions.

### 3. Reference
- The normative implementation model. The Golden References that prove the architecture is technically viable.

### 4. Engineering
- The scaling out of the architecture into dozens of Business Modules using the Production Engineering platforms.

### 5. Production
- The deployment of the artifacts into the live environment.

### 6. Operations
- Day-2 maintenance, monitoring, SRE practices, and incident management.

### 7. Evolution
- Refinement via the Continuous Architecture loop based on operational feedback.

### 8. Retirement
- The formal, controlled shutdown of an architectural component (`EA-0166`), completing the lifecycle.
