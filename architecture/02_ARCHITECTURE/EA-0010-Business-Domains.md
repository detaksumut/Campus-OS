# Business Domains

Campus OS is partitioned into high-level Business Domains to align with organizational structure.

1. **Admission Domain:** Handles the end-to-end lifecycle of a prospective student until enrollment.
2. **Academic Domain:** The central nervous system of the university, handling student progression, curriculum, scheduling, and graduation.
3. **Learning Domain:** Focuses on the delivery of education (LMS, Online Classes, Materials).
4. **Research Domain:** Manages research grants, proposals, and community service.
5. **Publication Domain:** Manages the editorial and publishing workflow for journals and articles.
6. **Academic Credential & Certification Domain:** A comprehensive credential ecosystem managing certifications, micro-credentials, digital badges, competency passports, and credential registries.
7. **Finance Domain:** Manages student billing (UKT/SPP) and institutional accounting.
8. **Human Capital Domain:** Manages lecturer and staff lifecycles, BKD (Beban Kerja Dosen), and payroll.
9. **Asset & Facility Domain:** Manages physical and digital infrastructure.
10. **Library Domain:** Manages academic resources and circulation.
11. **Alumni Domain:** Manages post-graduation engagement and tracer studies.
12. **Compliance Domain:** Manages synchronization with national bodies (PDDIKTI) and accreditation standards.
