# Enterprise Capability Map

The Capability Map defines "WHAT" Campus OS does, independent of "HOW" it is implemented.

## 1. Core Academic Capabilities
- **Admission Management:** Online Registration, Selection, Interview, Re-registration.
- **Academic Management:** Curriculum Planning, Course Scheduling, KRS Validation, Transcripts.
- **Learning Management:** Virtual Classrooms, Material Repository, Assignments, CBT.

## 2. Research & Publication Capabilities
- **Research Management:** Grant Proposals, Reviewer Assignment, Monitoring.
- **Publication Management:** Article Submission, OJS Integration.

## 3. Academic Credential Platform
Designed to be compatible with global standards (Open Badges) and the regional APASIFIC ecosystem.
- Certification Scheme Management
- Competency Framework
- Assessment & Moderation
- Credential Registry
- Digital Badge Issuance
- Continuing Professional Development (CPD)
- Credential Renewal
- Credential Verification
- Academic Recognition
- Credential Analytics

## 4. Financial & Administrative Capabilities
- **Finance Management:** Invoicing, Payment Gateway Integration, Budgeting.
- **Human Capital (HR):** Lecturer & Staff Data, Attendance, Payroll.
- **Asset Management:** Building, Lab, Computer, Inventory Tracking.

## 5. Ecosystem Capabilities
- **Library Management:** Cataloging, Circulation, E-Books.
- **Alumni Management:** Tracer Study, Community Engagement.
- **Compliance & Reporting:** PDDIKTI Sync, Accreditation (LKPS/Borang), Executive Dashboard.
